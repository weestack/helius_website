Tell me what is broken

A short description is enough. Within one business day I will tell you what I think the problem is, roughly how long it takes, and whether you need me at all. Sometimes the honest answer is no.

Alexander@helius.dk

I reply within 1 business day · No contact forms, no tracking pixels, no CRM sequence.

Let’s talk about your infrastructure

Email is the only channel, and it reaches me directly, not a shared inbox. A rough description is fine; a sentence is enough to start. If your organisation needs an NDA, a data processing agreement or a supplier questionnaire first, say so, and I will deal with it before we discuss details. If something is happening right now, put “urgent” in the subject line. I read those first.

Worth including

  • What triggered this, in a sentence or two
  • Roughly how many servers, and which distributions or clouds
  • Your timeline, and any hard deadline
  • Who on your side will own the result after handover

Not required

  • A formal RFP. If you have one, I will answer it
  • An NDA to start. If you need one, I will sign yours or send mine
  • A budget figure. The first review has a fixed price, and larger work is quoted in writing
  • A tidy problem statement. A rough description is fine

What happens next

I read it and reply within one business day with what I think the problem is, roughly how long it takes, and whether you need me at all. Sometimes a two-hour conversation solves it, or another consultant is a better fit. If so, I will say so rather than stretch to fit the work.
